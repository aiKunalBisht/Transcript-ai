AI Meeting Analyzer for Japanese & Multilingual Business Communication

TranscriptAI is an AI meeting intelligence platform for analyzing meeting transcripts, generating summaries, extracting key points, detecting action items and decisions, and understanding Japanese, Hindi, English, and mixed-language business communication. Unlike a basic meeting summarizer, TranscriptAI analyzes the meaning and context behind what participants say, including indirect communication, Japanese business expressions, formality, meeting outcomes, and approval signals.

When a Japanese counterpart says 検討いたします ("we will consider it"), TranscriptAI can identify the phrase as a potential soft rejection or non-commitment signal, assign a confidence score, and explain the communication context instead of treating the sentence as a simple action item or approval.

AI Meeting Analysis Features

  • Meeting summary analysis — automatically generate concise summaries from long meeting transcripts
  • Key point extraction — identify the most important information, discussion points, and meeting highlights
  • Action item extraction — detect tasks, follow-ups, responsibilities, and next steps from conversations
  • Decision detection — identify approvals, rejections, pending decisions, conditional decisions, and unresolved topics
  • Meeting outcome analysis — classify meetings as Approved, Rejected, Conditional, Deferred, Pending, Informational, At Risk, or Unclear
  • Meeting context analysis — understand the surrounding meaning of statements rather than relying only on individual keywords
  • Conversation and communication analysis — identify important language patterns, signals, topics, and business communication cues
  • Meeting transcript analysis — transform raw transcripts into structured business insights

Japanese Business Communication Analysis

  • Nemawashi signals — detect indirect consensus-building language that can appear before decisions or soft rejection
  • Japanese soft rejection patterns — detect expressions such as 難しいかもしれません, 対応しかねます, 善処します and other indirect responses
  • Keigo formality analysis — analyze Japanese formality and politeness levels using morphological analysis
  • Japanese indirect communication — identify business expressions whose intended meaning may differ from their literal wording
  • はい / understanding vs approval — distinguish acknowledgement or understanding from actual agreement or approval
  • Approval gate detection — identify statements such as 稟議が必要です and other indications that additional approval is required

Hindi, English & Multilingual Meeting Intelligence

  • Hindi indirect communication — analyze expressions such as dekhte hain, kal pakka, kuch na kuch ho jayega and related contextual signals
  • English meeting analysis — summarize discussions, extract key points, identify decisions, and detect action items
  • Hinglish analysis — analyze Hindi written or spoken in Roman script alongside English
  • Multilingual meeting analysis — process conversations involving Japanese, Hindi, English, and mixed-language communication
  • Cross-cultural communication insights — provide additional context for business communication across languages and cultures

Privacy & Secure AI Processing

  • PII masking — names, phone numbers, email addresses, and other personal information can be masked before external LLM processing
  • Privacy-conscious meeting analysis — designed for use cases where transcript privacy and sensitive business information matter
  • APPI-aware processing — built with Japanese privacy considerations in mind

Supported Languages

Japanese (日本語) · English · Hindi (हिंदी) · Hinglish (Hindi in Roman script) · Bilingual Japanese-English · Trilingual Japanese-Hindi-English mixed meetings
